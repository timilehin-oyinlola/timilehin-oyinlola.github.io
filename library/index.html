You Are Who God Says You Are

Peace be unto you, beloved!

Before anything else, pause and let this settle in your heart: your identity is not found in your grades, your background, your mistakes, or what people say about you.

It is found in Christ.
You are a new creation. “Therefore if any man be in Christ, he is a new creature: old things are passed away; behold, all things are become new.” (2 Corinthians 5:17)

You are chosen. “But ye are a chosen generation, a royal priesthood, an holy nation, a peculiar people.” (1 Peter 2:9)

You are God’s masterpiece. “For we are his workmanship, created in Christ Jesus unto good works.” (Ephesians 2:10)

You are a child of God. “But as many as received him, to them gave he power to become the sons of God.” (John 1:12)

Today, walk in who you are. You are loved, you are called, and you are enough in Him.
